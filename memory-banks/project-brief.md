## The foundation document that shapes everything else

### Project Name
**ForHer.AI — Gift Finder**

### Core Mission
A gift recommendation app that helps men find thoughtful, well-matched gifts for the women in their lives — mom, wife, girlfriend, grandma, sister, daughter, friend.

**Core question the system answers: "What would make her say 'how did you know?'"**

### Why This Exists
Gift shopping for women causes disproportionate stress for men. The stress comes from:
- **Decision paralysis** — too many options, no filter for relevance
- **Fear of getting it wrong** — a bad gift feels worse than no gift
- **Generic defaults** — candles, flowers, gift cards feel lazy but safe
- **Time pressure** — most gift shopping happens under a deadline
- **Guessing games** — "she said she doesn't want anything" is never true

Every existing solution organizes gifts by category (jewelry, skincare, home) instead of by *person*. This app starts from the person, not the product.

### What This Is NOT
- Not a marketplace — recommends and links out, does not sell
- Not a generic gift list — every recommendation is profile-driven
- Not a price comparison tool — finds the right gift, not the cheapest
- Not an AI chatbot wrapper — structured and guided, not open-ended conversation

### Development Phases
1. **Phase 1 — The Conversation (CURRENT):** Landing page + core interaction loop. One page, one text box, one response. 3–5 personalized gift cards.
2. **Phase 2 — Refinement Loop:** Reject/refine picks, follow-up questions, conversational back-and-forth.
3. **Phase 3 — Recipient Agents:** User accounts, named recipient profiles, accumulated context, gift history.
4. **Phase 4 — Retention & Growth:** Occasion reminders, trending gifts, expanded DB, affiliate monetization.

### Phase 1 Exit Criteria
A user types a 1–3 sentence description and receives recommendations that feel genuinely tailored — not random, not generic. The page feels calm and encouraging to use.

### Scope Rules
Phase 1 is just the conversation. Explicitly out of scope until Phase 1 is solid:
- Recipient agents and persistence
- User accounts
- Saved profiles and gift history
- Occasion reminders
- Live trending data
- Affiliate links / monetization
- Mobile app (web-first)
